Legal Policies

This page is dedicated for Cellulose3D Interactive's legal policies regarding usage of our digital works, moderation of community content, usage of our documentation, and more.

Warning: We're not lawyers, but we feel that we are obligated to list and enforce these policies to prevent illegal activity and communication of radical ideologies that can destabilize or disrupt communities. We also do not like plagiarism

Content Code

Content code applies to community-made blog posts, documentation, and software hosted, distributed, or advertised on this website. "Digital works" refers to community-made blog posts, documentation, and software hosted, distributed, or advertised on this website.

You can send a formal complaint if a digital work violates our content code.

  1. Digital works can not include inflammatory content targeted towards an individual, group, religion, ethnics, race, or individual with disabilities.
  2. Digital works can not include or refer to political topics regarding political events.
  3. Digital works can not include controversial topics.
  4. Digital works can not promote illegal activities.
  5. Digital works can not contain Not Safe For Work (NSFW) content.
  6. Digital works must limit profanity as much as possible.
  7. Digital works can not include photos of people without permission from those people.
  8. Authors must have permission for including any and all intellectual property in their digital works, even if those digital works comply with Fair Use laws.
  9. Digital works can not contain sensitive personal information, including (but not limited to) e-mail addresses, phone numbers, house addresses, postal code, Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, biometric data, etc.
  10. Digital works can not include references to the LUNX.cast team, KDF_graphics, and any drama about those groups of people.
  11. These rules are made for compliance with Indonesian law on Information and Electronic Transactions (UU ITE) and Ministerial Regulation No. 5/2020 (Permen Kominfo No. 5/2020 / Komdigi guidelines). Duplicate rules from the above are not listed.
    1. Digital works can not promote, facilitate, include links to, or reviewing online gambling, or any random chance activities.
    2. Digital works can not include content deliberately made to maliciously defame Cellulose3D, any group of people, religion, ethnics, race, or individual.
    3. Digital works can not spread misinformation designed to cause public unrest, or dangerous fake medical information.
    4. Digital works are prohibited from including unlicensed financial schemes (illegal pinjol / illegal lending, crypto scams).
    5. Digital works are prohibited facilitating sale of illegal firearms, explosives, or illegal wildlife trade.
    6. Digital works are prohibited from including content promoting terrorist organizations, violent extremism, separatism, or radical propaganda aimed at destabilizing national security.

Digital works violating the Content Code will be removed from our website.

Digital Works Reuse Policy

This policy applies to original digital works made by Cellulose3D Interactive, including (but not limited to) graphical artwork, music, software, and more. In this section, Digital works refer to said graphical artwork, music, and software.

  1. Verbatim distributions of digital works are not allowed without permission.
  2. Commercial use of digital works is prohibited for third-parties.
  3. Third-parties who distribute digital works shall give legal attribution to the authors and other parties with rights to said digital works.
  4. Digital works cannot be used for the following:
    1. Defaming Cellulose3D, authors of digital works, and other parties with rights to said digital works,
    2. Discrimination towards individuals(including Digital Works authors), groups of people (including Cellulose3D Interactive), race, religion, ethnics, people with protected traits, minorities, and people with disabilities.
    3. Promoting or facilitating illegal activities
    4. Communicating controverial topics, including politics, sensitive topics, etc.

Fair Use Policy

We allow reuse of content that complies with Fair Use laws, allowing usage of our digital works for criticism and educational purposes.
